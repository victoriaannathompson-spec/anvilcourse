What is Wallet Recovery?

Cryptocurrency wallet recovery is the process of restoring access to a digital wallet when the owner has lost their private key, seed phrase, wallet file, or password. Unlike traditional banks, blockchain wallets have no central authority — if you lose access, there is no forgot password button. This is where our forensic specialists come in.

Common Scenarios We Handle

  • Lost or forgotten seed phrase — including partial seed phrase reconstruction using cryptographic permutation methods
  • Corrupted or damaged wallet files — wallet.dat corruption and partial file recovery from damaged drives
  • Forgotten wallet passwords — advanced decryption techniques for encrypted wallet files
  • Old or abandoned wallets — wallets created years ago with lost credentials
  • Deleted wallet applications — forensic data recovery from device storage

Supported Networks

Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Cardano, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Chain, and 60+ others. All major wallet clients including MetaMask, Exodus, Electrum, Trust Wallet, Coinomi, and Atomic Wallet.

SIM-Swap Attack Prevention

Security · March 2026 · 8 min read

SIM-swap fraud has surged over 300% in 2025 to 2026. Attackers specifically target crypto holders using social engineering against mobile carriers.

What is a SIM-Swap Attack?

An attacker convinces your mobile carrier to transfer your phone number to a SIM card they control. Once they have your number, they can bypass SMS-based two-factor authentication on your exchange accounts, email, and other services — enabling full account takeover within minutes.

How Attackers Execute SIM Swaps

  • Social Engineering Carrier Staff — calling customer support with enough personal information to convince a rep to make the swap
  • Corrupt Insider Access — in some cases, attackers bribe carrier employees directly
  • Phishing for Account Credentials — gaining access to your carrier online account to initiate the swap without calling

Defensive Steps That Actually Work

  • Set a SIM Lock PIN with Your Carrier — a mandatory PIN required for any account changes
  • Switch to App-Based 2FA — replace SMS 2FA with Google Authenticator or a hardware security key
  • Use a Dedicated Email for Crypto — a separate email account secured with a hardware key, used only for exchange accounts
  • Remove Your Phone Number from Exchange Accounts — use authenticator apps instead of SMS for all crypto-related 2FA

Partial Seed Phrase Recovery

Recovery · February 2026 · 10 min read

Losing even a few words of your seed phrase does not necessarily mean permanent loss. With enough known words, cryptographic reconstruction may be possible.

Understanding BIP39 Seed Phrases

Most cryptocurrency wallets use the BIP39 standard, which generates a seed phrase of 12 or 24 words drawn from a fixed dictionary of exactly 2,048 words. Each BIP39 seed phrase includes a checksum — a mathematical verification mechanism. This dramatically reduces the number of combinations that need to be tested when some words are missing.

Recovery Feasibility by Missing Words

  • 12-word phrase with 1 missing word — 2,048 combinations. Recoverable in seconds.
  • 12-word phrase with 2 missing words — approximately 4 million combinations. Recoverable in minutes with our GPU cluster.
  • 24-word phrase with 3 missing words — approximately 8.5 billion valid combinations. Recoverable in hours to days.

Warning

Never enter your partial seed phrase into any online tool. Scammers specifically target people who have lost seed phrase words, promising recovery in exchange for the words they know — then draining the wallet. Always verify any recovery service before sharing wallet information.

Damaged Hardware Wallet Recovery

Hardware · December 2025 · 9 min read

Physical destruction of a hardware wallet does not necessarily mean permanent loss. We have recovered assets from devices that were underwater, exposed to fire, and physically crushed.

Recovery After Water Damage

Water damage is the most common hardware wallet emergency we see. If your device has been submerged: DO NOT attempt to turn it on — this is critical. Remove it from water, gently dry the exterior, and place it in a bag of silica gel packets. Contact us immediately. Water-damaged devices have an approximately 75% recovery success rate when they reach us without being powered on.

Recovery After Fire or Heat Damage

Fire creates more complex challenges, but the secure element chips in hardware wallets can survive temperatures up to 400 degrees Celsius in many cases. Our lab can perform chip-off extraction — physically removing the storage chip from the damaged PCB and reading it directly using specialised equipment in our cleanroom facility.

The Chip-Off Process

Chip-off extraction is a complex forensic procedure performed in our ISO-certified cleanroom. It involves non-destructive assessment under microscopy, micro-soldering to extract the storage chip, programming a custom interface adapter, reading raw flash memory data, and cryptographic analysis to reconstruct private keys.

Crypto Phishing in 2026

Fraud · November 2025 · 6 min read

In 2025, phishing attacks accounted for over $2.3 billion in crypto losses globally. AI-generated content makes many attacks nearly indistinguishable from legitimate communications.

The New Generation of Crypto Phishing

  • Spear Phishing — attackers research targets on social media before crafting personalised messages that appear to come from your exchange, mentioning recent transactions by amount and date
  • Fake Wallet Popups — browser extensions that mimic MetaMask or inject fake wallet connection popups into DeFi sites
  • AI Deepfake Scams — AI-generated video calls impersonating exchange representatives or financial advisors to convince victims to share seed phrases
  • Discord and Telegram Impersonation — fake admins in crypto project communities sending direct messages claiming to fix wallet sync errors or offering exclusive whitelist spots

How to Protect Yourself

  • Never enter your seed phrase anywhere online — no legitimate service ever needs it
  • Bookmark exchange URLs and only access them from bookmarks, never email links
  • Use a dedicated browser profile for all crypto activity
  • Enable hardware key authentication on all exchange accounts
  • If something feels off — it probably is. Stop and verify independently.

DeFi Protocol Recovery

DeFi · October 2025 · 11 min read

DeFi hacks resulted in over $3.7 billion in losses in 2025. While recovery is more complex than centralised exchange cases, blockchain tracing and legal pressure have produced significant recoveries.

Is Recovery Possible?

Unlike centralised exchange hacks, DeFi exploits involve immutable blockchain transactions. However, recovery pathways do exist. Many exploiters make operational security errors that allow their real identity to be traced through on-chain analysis and OSINT. When stolen funds eventually reach a centralised exchange for cash-out, law enforcement subpoenas can freeze and return them. Some protocol exploiters also accept bounty negotiations in exchange for returning the majority of funds.

Recovery Pathways

  • Attacker Operational Errors — many exploiters make mistakes allowing real identity tracing through on-chain analysis
  • Funds Move to CEX — when stolen funds reach a centralised exchange, law enforcement subpoenas can freeze and return them
  • White Hat Negotiation — some protocol exploiters accept bounty negotiations in exchange for returning the majority of funds
  • Insurance Protocol Claims — if you held coverage on Nexus Mutual, InsurAce, or similar DeFi insurance protocols, you may have a valid claim

What We Need to Begin

The affected wallet addresses, the protocol involved, the approximate date and time of the exploit, and any transaction hashes you have. Contact us for a free initial assessment.
